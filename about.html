About Us

Sowing dreams, growing landscapes

R Gunasekaran Proprietor - Divyasree Gardeners The visionary leadership of R Gunasekaran , who has been at the helm since 40 years, has played a crucial role in the growth and success of the company. With a deep passion for plants and sustainability, R Gunasekaran has ensured that Divyasree Gardeners thrives on innovation, quality, and personalized customer care. Over the years, Divyasree Gardeners has built a strong reputation for offering a diverse range of high-quality gardening tools, plants, and accessories, all designed to enhance the gardening experience. The business has continually adapted to changing customer needs, offering expert advice, environmentally-friendly solutions, and products that promote healthy and vibrant gardens.
